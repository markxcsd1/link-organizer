🎮 Game Backlog Bot

Share a game link on Telegram — it lands in your Notion backlog, fully tagged.
Trailer / review / store link→ Steam · IGDB · Groq→ Notion “To Play”

Send a trailer, a review, or a store page and the bot identifies the game, pulls its developer, genres, platforms and release date, finds a store page and a review, and asks how hyped you are before saving.

A FastAPI serverless function on Vercel. The backend lives in the game_bot package — see the source for the architecture.
